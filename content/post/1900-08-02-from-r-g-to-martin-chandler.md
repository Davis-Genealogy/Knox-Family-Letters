From: R.G., To: Martin Chandler  
Mailed From: Atwater, Illinois on August 2

Martin Chandler  
Pearl, Ill

Well Mister,  
When are you going to have a game. I will be there Tuesday not before. Cannot do it.  
For now,  
R.G.
