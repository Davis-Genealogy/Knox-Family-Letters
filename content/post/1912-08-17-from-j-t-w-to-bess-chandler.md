Mailed From: Hannibal, MO. on August 17, 1912

Miss Bessie Chandler  
Lousiana, MO.

Dear Miss B.,  
I was delighted to hear from you. I’m living here by Gump. You think I’m a liar but I’m not. It just happened.  
J.T.W.  
2108 Midtown St.
