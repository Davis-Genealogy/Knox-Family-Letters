From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler  
Mailed From: Newport News, Va. on April 7, 1919

Corp. MW. Chandler  
6th Casual Co  
Camp Hill – Va.  
Newport News

Mr. E.H. Chandler  
Pearl, Ill.  
Pike County

Hill, Va.  
6th Casual Co.  
April 7th, 1919  
Dearest Papa, Mama, and all  
Well guess I’m stuck here until next week – there is a shipment of men going out of here Saturday for Illinois and I tried to get in with them but they said it was full up and said I’d probably get out of here next week – Golly but it makes me sore. Here it is only Wednesday and I’ll probably be here until next wednesday. I heard over in germ nay that they were discharging men 24 hours after their arrival in the US. I landed here the 27th and wont be out until about the 14th of this month. I don’t see why in the dickens I can’t go with that bunch Saturday. This sure is a rotten camp. Don’t get hardly anything to eat -I’d like to tell this Newport News bunch just what I think of them.  
Don;t do a blessed thing but lay around all day in this darned camp. Lordy – I wish I was home. I get so sore every time I think about staying here another week that I’d like to cuss the whole bunch.  
Write me here. Think probably it will reach me before I leave.  
I haven’t written Grace and Lorin since landing cause I don’t know their address. Certainly hope you are all well. Guess I’ll be home someday.  
Lots of Love,  
Martin  
6th Casual Co  
Camp Hill  
Newport News, Va.
