Miss Ruby,  
C/O M.M. Davis,  
Emid, Oklahoma  
R.F.D.

Miss Ruby,  
I trust you are enjoying our cool nights. Can not hope you do the days. Do you not think that our summers beat those of Illinois? How is fighting Dick?  
H. Blasdel
