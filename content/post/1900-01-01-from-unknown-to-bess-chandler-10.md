From: Unknown, To: Bess Chandler

Miss Bess Chandler  
Pearl, Illinois

Dear Bess,  
Am working hard now in the store but not in the soda fountain. Had a dandy launch ride the other night. We are planning for a fishing trip. Hope it turns out better than the one on the 4th. Love to all. Ans. soon.
