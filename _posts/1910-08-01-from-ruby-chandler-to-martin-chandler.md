Mailed From: Enid, Oklahoma on August 1, 1910

Mr. Martin Chandler  
Pearl, Illinois

Dear Bro,  
Was so glad to get your letter. Let this suffice for my answer A good writer is concise and so am I. Do you still help Papa a great deal?  
Lovingly,  
Ruby
