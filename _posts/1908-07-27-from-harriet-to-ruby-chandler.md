From: Harriet, To: Ruby Chandler  
Postcard Info: Leap Year Series No. 1062  
Mailed From: Normal, Illinois on July 27, 1908

Miss Ruby Chandler  
Pearl, Illinois

Hello Ruby  
We miss the jolly bunch of girls which you helped to make.  
So long for now,  
Harriet
