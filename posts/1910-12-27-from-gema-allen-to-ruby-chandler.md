From: Gema Allen, To: Ruby Chandler  
Mailed From: Roadhouse, Illinois on December 27, 1910

Miss Ruby Chandler  
Pearl, Illinois

How is Pearl by this time? Ans. soon.  
Gema Allen

Porter &amp; Allen  
Dry Goods &amp; Clothing  
Roadhouse, Illinois
