From: Chas., To: Ruby Chandler

From: Chas., To: Ruby Chandler Postcard Info: B.B. London Series No. E.29. Printed in Germany Miss Ruby Chandler 217 N. School St. Normal, Illinois 7/18/1911 Received your card. Should like to have been there but will soon be running about over the same ground you are. I will be there on Sunday, the 16th. I guess I will get to say hello and goodbye. Chas.
