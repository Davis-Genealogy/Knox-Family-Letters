From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Ahrweiler, Germany on January 26 Ahrweiler, Germany Jan 26 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received letter from Papa day before yesterday dated Jan. 1st and very glad to get it and to know everybody is well. Was very anxious to know that Mama and Anna had recovered from the attack of influenza. Think I mentioned in a previous letter that I had received a bunch of my back mail including letters from Ruby, Grace, and Lorin. Not very much news to write. Persistent rumors indicate that we will leave next month. Most of the rumors sound very plausible and I’m beginning to put a little faith in them. Certainly would love to get home. I’m still on the same job. Business has been rather heavy last few days. Was notified few days ago that I would attend Liaison School at Coblens but have heard nothing definite about it since so don’t know whether or not I’ll go. Hope so anyway. The people here are as cordial as ever. They don’t seem to be suffering in the least. All the families I’ve seen so far have had plenty of food. I ate supper with a German family (Place where I room) other night and they had roast pork, something I hadn’t tasted for a year. Has been pretty cold last few days. Has snowed several times lately but not heavily. Having quite a bit of entertainment through the Red Cross, K of C and YMCA shoes have been pretty good. The 2nd Canadian Division met the 42nd (Rainbow) Division in an athletic contest short time ago. We won the meet by quite a margin. I attended the boxing bouts. We won 5 out of the 6 matches, scoring two knockouts. Had a very large attendance. Well must close for this time. Want to write to Grace tonight. Wrote to girls at Wichita several days ago. Hope this finds you all well. I’m feeling fine and dandy. Write often. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Grace Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Grace Chandler Mailed From: France on January 22 France Jan 22nd Dearest Grace and all, has been some time since I’ve written so thought I’d write few lines this pm while nothing to do. Had a pleasant voyage across the Atlantic. Was on the water Christmas and New Years. Am in the Hospital here now with measles. Come in the 15th. Am getting along OK and feel good. Hows Audrey getting along? My I’d love to see her as well as rest of you. It hasn’t been very cold here past 10 days, but has rained considerably. I expect to join my company in about a week. Well I can’t say very much so will close. Be sure and write soon as you can. Hope you are all feeling well. Lovingly, Martin Address: Pvt. M.W. Chandler Co “B” 1st Fed Bn St. Corpo. American Evped. From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey Mr. E. H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike Co 5th Dearest papa mama, and all Received 2 letters from papa day before yesterday. Awful glad to get them. Was just starting home from club and thought would drop you couple lines right quick. Nothing new to write. Don’t know when we will leave. Nobody seems to know. May be here until Christmas and again may leave within the week. We’re all prepared to leave immediately. They fixed up the allotment for me today. You will get 20.00 about 1st of Nov. and each month from then on for 1 year. I wont have 20 myself to send it home now. Seems like it slips through my fingers just as I get it. Hope everybody is well. Am in hurry just now so will stop and write more some other time. Feeling fine. Lovingly, Martin ...

January 1, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Ruby Chandler, To: Manca

From: Ruby Chandler, To: Manca Monday A.M. Dearest Manca, I will write just a note at this time and enclose any letters that have come since I wrote last. Bess said she’d write herself today so she can tell you anything I have left out. Bess came yesterday (Sun.) noon and Papa was at home too so we had a good visit. She looks fine. She brought each of us something new and brought you a new dress which we’ll make and send you. I want you to visit until your pass limit and rest assured that perhaps all things wont go to “rack and ruin.” Anyway we might get some sewing done for you under stress of your necessity especially while visiting and you know how much that knowledge of necessity can cause us to accomplish at times so we’ll try to get another little package to you before long. I’m sure I appreciate and thank Aunt Annie’s kindness in giving you such a fussy dress. That’s just like her. I was talking to several down at the soldier’s banquet about Uncle’s death and they all had praises for Aunt Annie. Clem Hanback talked as if she Aunt had been her bosom friend. I’ll send you the papers, too. Martin sent his card here for registration as you’ll see by his letter. Papa had the garden in perfect condition also the lawn and has poled the beans, latticed the tomato plants and planted radishes. Annie has been all I could wish for the past several days. Love to all. Lovingly, Ruby ...
