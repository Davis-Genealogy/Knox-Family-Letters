From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed On: July 31, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ills Pike Co July 31st Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received letter from papa day before yesterday and one today and mighty glad to get them. General Representative of Russia was here today reviewing our troops. They had a big parade, about 12000 soldiers. Gee it sure looked pretty. As far back as I could see was column after column of soldiers. The signal corps was up toward the front, mounted. I had a pretty good house. By golly from the way paper reads the general had better beat it to Russia. Haven’t heard anything about how long will be here but couldn’t be surprised if didn’t stay here until next spring. It’s pretty warm here in day time but the night are always cool and have to cover up. Thane good thing. Awful glad the girls got their pass to Dunlap. I’ll write to them tonight in this company from Illinois. Sure seems good to see dome one from up there. What is Ralph doing? Hope he gets a job OK. I saw in paper where all the switches around Chad were on a strike. Hope it doesn’t delay the girls trip any. Well I’m in a hurry. Got to get ready to stand retreat. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

June 9, 1917 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Sanderson on June 14, 1917 Sanderson June 13-17 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received 2 letters from papa today and sure glad to get them. Guess it’s been quite a while since I’ve written. Been feeling pretty glum lately but OK now. It’s blooming hot can’t do anything. I’m sending my resignation in tomorrow and am going to work for Western Union. May come home. I’d like to get away from this hot country, nothing but barren hills. Had big sand storm today but I was in bed and didn’t bother me any. Had my ears full of sand when I got up. Am sending you picture under separate cover. But you were all glad to see Bess, wish I could see you all. Suppose Mama will be home in a few days. Will mail three pictures. Wish you’d give Grace one. Started to mail one to Morrisonville but wasn’t sure she was there. Guess Lucerne will have a good time in Granite. Whats Knox doing anyway? Guess he is doing lots of swimming. I haven’t been swimming since left California. Well don’t know any news. Everything quiet down here. Wont leave here for week or so yet. Lovingly, Martin ...

June 4, 1917 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Sanderson on May 31, 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all I received three letters from papa and one from mama,enclosing Lorin’s and was sure tickled to get them. First mail I had received since leaving Valentine. Sure was too bad about that cyclone. Hope no more of them come along through here. Glad that one didnt hit Pearl. From looks of things I’m going to be here for couple months unless they send a man to relieve me and let me come to El Paso. I got all the clippings Papa sent. Wouldn’t mind being with Pershings expedition if could get a good job. I went over to court house this PM and registered before County Clerk and Mailed it to registrar Pearl, care Sheriff Pittsfield. That way clerk said to address it. I enclosed stamped envelope so he could mail me a receipt. I must be there by the 5th. Should reach there in plenty of time. Golly its sure hot down here. Temperature of 110. Wish I was home. I couldn’t quit very well now if I wanted to account them being so short of operators and couldn’t get relief. This is a very large place. Like it better than Valentine though. It pays 7.50 more on the month. Is Aunt Sarah feeling OK? Hope so. She always was so good to me. Suppose Knox has stumped his toes a dozen times or so. Does he wash his feet before he goes to bed? Hope Lorin gets the job he mentioned. Gee wish I could see that kid of Grace’s. I’ll bet she is some baby. Have any of you been fishing this season yet? I’d like to be there and go squirrel hunting with Papa this season. Golly wish I was home. I only have one dispatcher to work with here. Get along fine with him. I saw him at Del Rio while back. Seems to be a nice man. Sure are a good bunch dispatchers and chiefs at El Paso. They all treat me good there and would show me about different wires and how to adjust a quad. I worked on a quad most of time I was there. I’d be sending to Houston and Houston would be sending to Opr on other side of me all at same time on same wire. Kinda funny how it works but it does. Four men can work on the same wire, two sending and two receiving. Sure had lots messages. Run about 700 with Houston each day but I didn’t have to do very much. Just about 4 hours steady work with Houston and two hours with Los Angeles and San Francisco. Well don’t know anything more to write. Sure glad to get that bunch of letters today. Mama hope you write again pretty soon. Papa averages a letter nearly every day and by golly they sure look good. Hope everybody is well. Hope Vinnie gets well OK. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Langtry, Texas on May 9, 1917 Langtry, Tex 5/9th 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Got her evening of 7th. This sure is some town. One store and 2 or 3 restaurants. Not a very heavy job. Pays $87.50 and commissions all told ought to make it pay 100 but I’ll only be here two or three days longer at least. I can stand on platform and throw across the Rio Grande into Old Mexico. Only about a hundred yards to River. Saw a whole bunch of Mexicans over there yesterday. Don’t know what they were doing. I telegraph from 9 am to 3 pm. Express Commission ran $8.00 last month. Ha. He said it was a very poor month. Don’t have very much to do. Have oil tanks here and have to contend with that but its nothing like Valentine. I kinda wish he hadn’t out me on Valentine agency cause now I’ll have to be running around working relief agencies and can’t stay on my regular job and its more expensive to be running around here and there. Do hope Margaret is well. Awful glad to hear Lucerne made her grades. How did Knox make it? Too bad Margaret couldn’t take examination. Did I mention receiving Papas and Mamas letters enclosing Bess’? Anyway I did and mighty glad to get them. Haven’t heard anything from Cra yet but expect to in few days. Don’t suppose I could get a pass up there anyway. I got a letter from MK 2 and they said they would be glad to furnish me pass San Antonio to St. Louis provided it was ok with me that they notify this road they had issued such transportation as required by I.C.C. Upon inquiry I found out that if I get pass from MK 2 that the RHASA will blacklist me from their road. They don’t allow their employees to write for their own passes without taking up with them so that knocks me out of pass cause I don’t want to have a bad record here. I don’t see why in the dickens they should care who I get pass from. Guess if they wont furnish transportation I’ll ride the cabooses. Wouldn’t take me very long to get there. Sure will be glad when this war is over. I’m going to work in Western Union this summer sure. It’s good for $125.00 anytime. They have established an 8 hour day and overtime on Sundays now. That makes W.U. pretty good. Well must stop and Get busy. Better write me at Valentine. Expect to be back there by time you get this but if go someplace else can have my mail forwarded. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Valentine, Texas on May 5, 2917 Mr. E. H. Chandler Pearl, Ill. Pike County M. W. Chandler Valentine, Texas Valentine, May 5th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Papa’s dandy letter today. Sure good to see them come so often. Hope Ralph straightens out and gives good services. I’d hate to see him get discharged. I may write JLM pretty soon and see what he can offer. Wouldn’t mind coming up to Coa if could get enough work. Think I’ll come anyway. If he will put me to work and then go to work in Western Union first time I get a chance. I’ll let you know what he says soon as I had from him. I’m getting pretty tired of this country. Easy job but don’t like the climate. Golly prices are sure high. Darn food trusts. It’s just a drought. I’ll bet theres no good reason at all for such exorbitant prices. They just use the war as an excuse. How’s Knox getting along. Knox why the Dicken’s don’t you write once in awhile. Hope Margaret is well by now. Glad she didn’t get Diphtheria. I wrote Sam a letter other day. Guess he will be kinda surprised to get it. Don’t seem possible you are having so much rain there. Been wanting a rain here for last 4 months. I can’t remember when it has rained here. Everything dry as a chip. has Grace gone to Louisville yet? Gee I’d love to see Audrey.Is Lorin still selling Oliver’s? Well i don’t know much to write. Hope to see you all before many days. Lovingly, Martin P.S. Just wrote J.L.M. for a job. Let you know what he says soon as I hear from him. ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Eliojah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: El Paso, Texas on June 10, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois St. Blanca January 10, 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all; Received 2 letters from Papa today. One via valentine and I was glad to get them both. Didn’t think you’d be able to get one to St. Blanca so quick. Nothing new to write. Getting along O.K. I called up both last night and asked them what they wanted me to do in case one called while I was working with other and they said just use my own judgement and if I pay no attention to them they get sore. I’m hitting the ball here pretty lively. Keep busy every minute of the night. The depo I trick open copied a 19 for a IP 2/5 train this pm to meet another IP No. 2 train at Fort Hancock. No. 2 got to Fort Hancock and waited 2 hours for the 2nd 5 to show up but they didn’t so he called up Dispr. on phone and went on. 2nd No. 5 was in a blind siding down the line. They claimed they didn’t get the order. Disp. came after open about it and open looked for his clearance and he had it non clearance so it will be up to the train creed. Well, must stop. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: England on January 9, 1917 Somewhere in England Jan 9th Dearest Folks, Thought I’d write few lines tonight while have time but can’t tell you much except that I’m feeling OK and had a pleasant voyage. It’s pretty cold here but not any worse than New Jersey. Am not allowed to mention hardly anything so don’ expect to learn anything about the war from me. Rode on an English train other night. They’re a great deal different from ours. They are built in compartments. Each compartment sealing 8 men (3rd class). No way of getting from one compartment to another except by getting off and entering another. Just like traveling in a carriage. Had a good stateroom on the steamer and didn’t get a bit seasick. I’ll write you again in few days and don’t worry if you don’t hear from me regularly. Hope you are all well- Lots Love Martin Address Exactly Like This: Martin W.
