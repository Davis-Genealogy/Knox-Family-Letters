January 5, 1915 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailing Address: Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike County Return Address: M. Chandler COB 1st BSC Mammouth Parking 9 am Indianapolis, Mo. 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, & all En route to Mammouth Park, Mo. Cmae through K.C. & St. Louis both but was only in each place 15 minutes and was way down in yards. We weren’t allowed to leave train at all. Just now pulling into Maple. Intended to write you from KC but no chance. We came through St. Louis at 11 pm last night. Rock bends El Paso to St. Louis and then St. Louis to destination. Should reach New Jersey about night. All the trains are taking siding for us and we have had a clear track since leaving Mt. Bliss. Delayed occasional by broken tunnel on one of the car horses and then we have to unload them every 3 hours and let them rest 8 hours. All the little towns in Missouri and Kansas had word of our coming and school was dismissed and they were all down to station with flags to wave at use. One girl passed out a basket of cigarettes. from way they are rushing us am inclined to think we will go on over to France although some say we are going to camp in NJ for winter. We have dandy pullman and good meals. Having fine trip. Didn’t come through Wichita. Wanted to bad too. Came through Hutchinson, Kans. Well I’m in hurry so can mail this here in Maples cause don’t know when we will stop again. Will write you just as soon as get to New Jersey and give you my address. Love to all Martin (over) P.S. Suppose you get the Battalion picture my picture isn’t a bit good. Can hardly be recognized. Am in the sixth grow from the right and third row right next to the big black horse on cart. ...

December 16, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey October 18 Oct 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received papas letter yesterday and Bess’ dandy letter today and mighty glad to get them. I came over other night and wrote but forgot to mail it so guess it has been quite a while since you’ve heard from me. The 11th Reserve battalion left for France this morning. We got our lockers back this am also got stoves so guess we will camp here this winter from looks of things but of course can’t tell for sure. I think they intended to send one battalion over and hadn’t decided who to send hence our preparedness but now since the 11th have gone we’re likely to stay here for some time. I’ve been getting the Pike Country Republican lately. Who’s sending it? It has news from boys at Camp Taylor. Saw Wade’s and Jesse’s picture in it. Tell Parish I’ll write the Tribune a letter relative Army Life as far as I knew. Don’t know anything about it except Signal Corps Branch. Awful glad to hear Knox’s arm is healing. Hope he doesn’t fall from some place and break it again before it mends. We’ve been out on target range 3 days out of this week and go again tomorrow. I can shoot pretty well. Scored 76 out of 100 first time. I even tho one of the automatic pistols. Lots pretty good shots in this company. Is Aunt Sarah still at our house? They are offering liberty bonds to our company on installment plans. We have about 75 men and they’re taken $4200.00 so far. I can’t take any account the allotment home. Have o pay at rates $10.00 per month and thats all I’m getting. By the way, you’ll get first payment 1st of December. Captain said it was paid one month behind June all the time and then you’ll get the last one a month after I’m out of army. Gee Margaret is getting rather popular. Having bunch of fellows wanting to call on her. It’s pretty chilly here. heavy frosts each morning. Well I’ll stop now and go back to camp. Pretty tired tonight. On our feet all day today fixing stoves for the company. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Unknown Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Unknown Female Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on September 3 Santa Margarita, CA Sept 3 Dear Little Sister, Got your letter this morning together with Margaret’s and was sure glad to get it. By golly I wish you’d all write every day. Hang on to the iron kiddo. It will make a woman out of you. Gee that sure was a whole bunch of tough luck about that earache. Did you try warm milk and Laudanum? That’s what Mama used to do for me when I was a kid. You want to read slow on this so it will last longer cause I haven’t got time to write very much. Well I gotta stop. Write me right away quick, Yrs, Martin ...

September 3, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on July 22 Santa Margarita July 22nd Dearest Papa and All, Just received letters from you, mama, Lucerne and Margaret fe minutes ago and was sure glad to get the. Wish they would come that way all the time. I wrote yo Mama few minutes ago and wrote to her day before yesterday in Morrisonville so guess she will get it soon after she gets there. Getting along OK on the job. Coping about 50 train orders last night and had 43 Expense Bills. Handle mail or 3 trains and sell tickets for 3 trains so there’s generally enough to keep me busy. Like the job fine though. Theres lots of fellows here wanting to lay off and can’t get relief. M en are awful scarce but in the winter time they close so many offices that it throws a whole bunch of men on extra board but extra work looks pretty good for this winter and if I earn to handle an agency by that time will be assured of almost steady work. These agencies pay big money too. Do you and Knox get any fish? That was a pretty good string you caught the other day. Bet Knox was tickled to yank them out. Wish I was there so could go squirrel hunting few times. Gee the girls sure are bathing lots berries. Hope Ruby is feeling better. Golly I haven’t heard a piano for so long I’ve forgotten how one sounds. Hope Ralph will be able to go back to work soon. Too bad Micky had to take sick. Well the train is coming now that I want to get this off on so will close. Wish all of you would write. Hope everyone is OK. Will send Annie a card right away. Love to all, Martin ...

July 22, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: France on April 11 France April 11th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all No mail since last writing. Received letter from Bess today dated at Dunlap. Sure was glad to get it. That’s letter number 3 received since being here. Nothing new to write, or rather, lots news to write but can’t write it. Having lovely weather. Guess Aunt Anna and Ruby are there now. Bess said they were going to meet in K.C. and come to Pearl together. I’d like to be there myself. Am still batching with other fellows. Like it fine. I’m on a Radio job here. Get Paris and Berlin press each day. Get about 1000 words from Paris relative military situation. German don’t put out much news. I’m getting fleshy on our own cooking. Most meals consist of Ouefs, Pomme de terries, Coffee, Bun de pain, and confiturerie. You see I’m learning to cook in the French style-Ha. We only have 2 meals a day – one about 10:30 am and one about 5:00 pm. We 5 fellows sure have a great time. At least we enjoy ourselves as much as possible under the existing conditions. Lots singing, joking, etc. Might win myself a home with a little French girl if I could speak and understand French well enough. I get a smile once in a while and then when she starts talking I’m lost so then she gets peeved cause all her words were wasted. Sure is a great life. We have things fixed pretty nice in our radio station. Good bunks, etc. Dad how goes everything on the “only way”? Did you all get a new schedule yet? They should get a raise by all means. We have a sergeant in charge of this station. Sure is a Prince besides a very efficient man. If I ever get a chance I’ll have some pictures taken and send home. Intended doing it before leaving the states. Hows fishing around there? I haven’t seen a fish for so long I’ve forgotten how they look. I’ve seen a few rabbits over here. Only game I’ve seen. Well, must close and get supper. All of you write often. Am feeling great. Hope you are all well. Love, Martin Pvt. M.W. Chandler Co “C” 117th F.B.S.C. A.E.F. ...
