From: Allen, To: Ruby Chandler Mailed From: Wichita, Kansas on August 1, 1911 Miss Ruby Chandler Pearl, Illinois Dear Ruby, Got your card but not your letter. I am writing this at the Y.M.C.A. bldg., I moved here today. I have about 100 pictures to show you. I took them on my Colorado trip. Hope yo hear from you soon. Allen
