From: Martin Chandler, To: Margaret Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Margaret Chandler Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey on November 2, 1917 Miss Margaret Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike County Nov 2nd, 1917 Dearest Margaret, Received your letter some time ago and sure glad to get it. Wish you’d write often. Hows school coming? Guess you’re having a great time playing basket ball. Gee it will soon be Christmas. Seems an age since I’ve spent an Xmas at home. Sure hope I’ll be at home some Christmas. Don’t know where I’ll be this Christmas. Kinda looks as thought we were going to leave before soon. But can’t tell much about it. Didn’t expect to be here now. Sure is cold here. Having lots rain too. Makes it awful disagreeable. I’m sure having a peach of a time in the kitchen. Lots of work to do. Get up at 5:30 am and work until 6:30 pm. Wash dishes, pans, peel spuds, onions, and do a little bit of everything. Carry coal and cut wood each day. I’ll be off tomorrow night and I’ll sure be glad of it. Wrote Bess last night and put a 2 cent stamp on it so guess she will have to pay a penny to get it out. Didn’t think about having to put 3 cent on it. When are you going to Morrisonville? How is Audrey? Golly Id sure love to see her and the rest of you all. Well I don’t know anything to write tonight so will stop. Be sure and write as often as you can cause I sure love to get the letters. Hope everybody is well. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Camp Alfred Vail, Litte Silver, New Jersey on October 8, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike County M.W. Chandler Co B 1st FBSC Little Silver, NJ Camp Alfred Vail Little Silver, NJ 9th 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received 3 letters from papa since last writing and mighty glad to get them. Was so sorry to hear Knox has broken his arm and I’ll be anxious to hear when he is OK again. That will keep him out of school too. Be sure and let me know how he gets along. Nothing much new to write except were all afraid we wont get to go to France for awhile. We’re all prepared to leave but nobody know anything about when we are going. Some think the company will be split up and sent to different places as non commissioned officers to drill the National army. Everybody thought we would leave immediately and its sure some disappointment to think we might not go. Can’t tell anything about it though. I’m on guard duty now. On at 5:30 pm yesterday and off at 5:30 this pm. Do guard duty 2 hours off four hours. It’s pretty cold here at nights but have good warm clothing and a dandy overcoat. I’ll sure be glad to get that sweater. Overcoats are so unhandy to go out to drill but got to wear something to keep warm and that sweater will make it fine and dandy. I just got away from the guard house for few mins to come over to YMCA and write. Hands are so cold can hardly write. I’ll be over at club tomorrow night and will write from there. Be sure and let me know how Knox makes it. Hope rest of you are all well. In case we leave unexpectedly will write you if permitted. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey on September 28, 1917 M.W. Chandler Co “B” 1st Field Battalion Camp Alfred Vail Little Silver, N.J. Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill. Pike County Camp Alfred, Vail 26th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Golly Havent received any mail since being here. Thought sure would have received a letter before now. Everything going along OK. Awful chilly here but otherwise like it OK. Haven’t heard anything definite as to when we will go to France but were sure going. I’m anxious to get over there. 25 Signal Corps men and 3 officers left today for Franc. 2 come over to the Signal Corps Club nearly every night. Good way to pass time away. Have a dandy Victrola and piano. All kinds books and magazines to read. Been doing lot of fatigue since coming here getting camp straightened out. I haven’t been on a horse since leaving Texas but will go out tomorrow on drill. How are the children coming in school? Is the apple season over yet papa? How much does Grace’s baby weigh now? Lordy I’d love to see her. Did she enter the contest at the state fair? Well guess I’ll go down and finish my story. Am reading “The Dark Star” by Chambers. Have to be back in camp by 11 pm. We all drew overcoats yesterday. The sure are dandy. Will get rest of our winter clothing right away. Fitzjarrellz is over here with me tonight. You know he is the fellow I mentioned from White Hall. Does Ruby remember him? He used to go with Lucille Knox. Well must stop. Hurry up and write. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Fort Bliss, Texas on August 27, 1917 M.W. Chandler Co “B” 1st F.B.S.C. Ft. Bliss Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike County Co “B” 1st FBSC Ft. Bliss Texas Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received 2 letters from papa since returning from hike last Saturday evening and sure glad to get them. We went up into New Mexico on the hike. Had a dandy time. The company was split up and strung out through the country and we had continuos wire communication which we put up ourselves. I stayed on duty until midnight. A and C company were along too. A company is the Radio outfit. They put up their wireless apparatus and were in communication with El Paso all the time. They also had a wire strung to our camp and out company relayed all messages for El Paso to the Radio Co and they sent them by wireless to El Paso and Ft. Bliss. The man who brought Papa’s letter to me yesterday saw that it was from Pearl and asked me if I knew anyone there. I told him I lived there and he said he was from White Hall. He sleeps in tent next to mine. He is a step brother of Pearl Ellis and said he remembered Ruby and Pearl were at their house one time. His name is Fitzjarrellz. He is Corporal in the company and a nice fellow. He also knows Uncle Will’s family at Manchester and used to go with Lucille. Sure surprised me to meet him. Said he had been at Pearl lots of times playing ball and coming to boat shows and knows lots of the fellows around Pearl. He’s the only fellow I’ve ran across close from home. Our battalion played baseball with 2nd telegraph battalion yesterday and beat them 7 to 3. Captain was sure pleased. I had m picture taken in ball suit with another fellow. Just a Kodak. Will send you one soon as they are finished. Mama did you ever find the Kodaks in my suit case? Well its just 5 min until drill time and I’ve been writing this in an awful hurry so could get it off this am. Hope everybody is OK. Well write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed On: July 31, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ills Pike Co July 31st Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received letter from papa day before yesterday and one today and mighty glad to get them. General Representative of Russia was here today reviewing our troops. They had a big parade, about 12000 soldiers. Gee it sure looked pretty. As far back as I could see was column after column of soldiers. The signal corps was up toward the front, mounted. I had a pretty good house. By golly from the way paper reads the general had better beat it to Russia. Haven’t heard anything about how long will be here but couldn’t be surprised if didn’t stay here until next spring. It’s pretty warm here in day time but the night are always cool and have to cover up. Thane good thing. Awful glad the girls got their pass to Dunlap. I’ll write to them tonight in this company from Illinois. Sure seems good to see dome one from up there. What is Ralph doing? Hope he gets a job OK. I saw in paper where all the switches around Chad were on a strike. Hope it doesn’t delay the girls trip any. Well I’m in a hurry. Got to get ready to stand retreat. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

July 3, 1917 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on September 18, 1916 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike Co Santa Margarita Sept 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Mama’s letter yesterday and haven’t heard from papa in a coons age. Sure was glad to get Mama’s letter. This is first time I’ve written for several days. Getting along OK and will be on this job at least a month longer and probably 2 months. Agent has gone to New York so regular 3rd trick man got his job leaving me here for while longer. Sure have a bunch of train orders to handle. Keeps me going sometimes. Getting pretty cool out there at night. Have to have a fire every night. See in the paper where they have rented the M.E. Church for Primary room. Thought they had built a new school house there or had added to the old one. See they’ve started on the ditch again. I’d like to see it. I’ve been going up to the school-man’s house nearly every night. We all went swimming other day. Gee they have swell schoolhouse here. Have piano, dance floor, opera house, and everything in it. Have some pretty swell scenery on the stage. It’s a single story building but has 4 rooms besides the opera and dance room. Sure is swell. Gee I sure was tickled when agent told me he was going to lay off 2 months. Felt pretty sure I’d get to stay on this job. Ruby is making a pretty good visit at Grace’s. Wish I was there. Well I must stop and get busy. Bell rings just about every 10 or 15 min for 9.00. Wish you’d all write a little more often. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: France on March 10 France Mar 10th Dearest Folks, Will write just few lines tonight while have good chance. Nothing new to write. Have received no mail yet. Suppose it will be several months before you can get a letter to me at this address. Requires quite a while to get an answer to a letter. Have moved since last writing. Would like to tell you of our locations. Things are pretty warm here. Been having some very nice weather here for past week. Hope it continues. I’ve been feeling very good. Get plenty to eat. Have a pretty good YMCA here. Affords a good place to write letters. Well must close and turn in. Am on K.P. now and have to get up at 4 am. Hope you are all feeling well. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin P.S. Dont have any of my letters put in “Tribune.” ...

March 10, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: France on February 28 Dearest Folks, Have been traveling about quite a bit since last writing you. Was transferred to the 117th F.B.S.C. Feb 12. Like the organization very well and if I had my mail, clothes, etc from the 1st FB would just as soon stay with this organization. My service record is still with my old company. Don’t know when I’ll get a pay. Haven’t received any pay since October. Would like very much to get the mail you’ve already sent to me at 1st FB. But don’t know whether or not it will be forwarded here. Been having some very rainy weather the past week. Guess this is the rainy season. First I’ve seen since being over here. Would like to tell you of our whereabouts but of course that’s forbidden. Am feeling very good. Only hope you are all feeling as well. Must stop for this time. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin Address: Pvt. Martin W. Chandler Co ‘C” 117 F.B.D.C. A.E.F. Via New York ...
