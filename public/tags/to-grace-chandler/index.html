From: J.M. Jones, To: Grace Chandler Mailed From: St. Louis on June 19, 1913 Oliver Type Writer Building 812 Pine Street. St. Louis June 19/1913 Miss Grace Chandler, Pearl, Illinois Dear Miss: We are in receipt f your letter of June 18th in which you have asked for commissions due on sale to J.W. Smith and H. Owings. The commission on the order of J.W. Smith is due, and check is being sent you under separate cover today. The commission on the sale to Herbert Owings will not be due until April 7th, 1914, at which time you will receive your commission check in accordance with the contract which was made with you. Yours very truly, J.W. Jones Assistant Manager ...

From: Unknown, To: Grace Chandler

From: Unknown, To: Grace Chandler Mailed From: Pearl, Illinois on November 28, 1912 My Dear Gracie, Not so much fun about that as there was in the beginning of your letter. But then the old era is so darn full of trains tonight, I had to come down and relieve the Opr tonight, while he went to dinner Ha. First my dear I want to mention tomorrow night. This weather one “guy” can’t have much luck on a speeder and that way I’d have to come over if I came. So if all is suitable with you I’d rather make it Friday instead of Thurs eve and I’ll come on 33 and back on the Chicago train and that way you’ll not have to ask me to leave at ten for I’ll have to go shortly before. Grace you know those trains are always some last an I’ll get to stay until your tired of me. Yes about your friend Mr. Lienhatrdt, I sat with him coming home on 12 Monday. He also told me he was with you at a party other night. made me kinda sore too cause I never get any invitations to the parties. Ha. Yes it was really me he saw on 12 – true I would have enjoyed a couple of days more at home but my dear there is seemingly no rest in sight and I must also add that truly, the most restful periods in my “career” is, when I am spending my evenings with you. And I look forward anxiously to those evenings. I wonder if I will be fortunate enough to spend a few more of them. Yes! When you mentioned that great subject (candy), seems to me as though I can recall a bet made some weeks ago, where in you were looser? And am counting big on tasting some real good fudge when you make it. Do remember me. And you are contemplating going to St. Louis eh! Looks like you’d put it off until next Saturday night and let me go along. We will take in a show and look around some. Honest – for I need to do the shopping stunt too. Here’s my trip Saturday- to Maybrew on 15- Marshall on 18 Sunday Slater on 24 Sunday-12 onto Roadhouse and back on 19- 8:30 am – Monday. Had a great time, met some dandy fellows at Marshall, also saw some girl friends. Wish you might have been along for Missouri (Saline County) is the place for fun. Ask “Jake” will mail this on #15 tomorrow and trust it will reach you in due time also meet your approval. Let here have a card on 16 dearie if ok for me to come up Friday evening. Darn if we have been together for 4 months. Save me a kiss too will you? French? Yes. You going to meet the train – 33? yes. No news here so I will leave it with you. Too bad about Smith. Ha. All yours, (Unreadable Scribble) 8:30 1st Wednesday ...
