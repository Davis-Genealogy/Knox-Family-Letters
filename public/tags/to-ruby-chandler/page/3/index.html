From: Pearl Ellis, To: Ruby Chandler

From: Pearl Ellis, To: Ruby Chandler Mailed From: Manchester, Illinois on January 19, 1911 Miss Ruby Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike County Thady’s Farm Jan 18 – 1911 My dear Ruby, Are you paying me back for the way I treated you dear? Well I don’t like it. I want to hear from you. Did you ever get my photo I sent to you before Christmas? I was afraid you had not, as I had not heard from you. How is everybody? Give my love to all the family. I was at Knox’s last Sunday. They are all well. Am getting a little tired of school. Had a fine time in Decater and Urbana during holidays. Love, Pearl ...
