From: F. Foreman, To: Ruby Chandler

From: F. Foreman, To: Ruby Chandler Mailed From: Chicago, Illinois in 1909 Picture Info: Field Museum and Wooded Island, Jackson Park, Chicago, Ill. Postcard Info: Published by A. C. McClurg & Co., Chicago, Ill. Made in U.S.A. Miss Ruby Chandler Milton, Illinois My Dear Ruby, Undoubtably, you think I have forgotten you ut I have not. Nothing like that in our family. You ought to be here and enjoy some of the good times with me. Edna is coming up zoo. How is school? By now, I should be glad to hear from you at any time. F. Foreman ...
