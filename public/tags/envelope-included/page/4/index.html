From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Camp Alfred Vail, Litte Silver, New Jersey on October 8, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike County M.W. Chandler Co B 1st FBSC Little Silver, NJ Camp Alfred Vail Little Silver, NJ 9th 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received 3 letters from papa since last writing and mighty glad to get them. Was so sorry to hear Knox has broken his arm and I’ll be anxious to hear when he is OK again. That will keep him out of school too. Be sure and let me know how he gets along. Nothing much new to write except were all afraid we wont get to go to France for awhile. We’re all prepared to leave but nobody know anything about when we are going. Some think the company will be split up and sent to different places as non commissioned officers to drill the National army. Everybody thought we would leave immediately and its sure some disappointment to think we might not go. Can’t tell anything about it though. I’m on guard duty now. On at 5:30 pm yesterday and off at 5:30 this pm. Do guard duty 2 hours off four hours. It’s pretty cold here at nights but have good warm clothing and a dandy overcoat. I’ll sure be glad to get that sweater. Overcoats are so unhandy to go out to drill but got to wear something to keep warm and that sweater will make it fine and dandy. I just got away from the guard house for few mins to come over to YMCA and write. Hands are so cold can hardly write. I’ll be over at club tomorrow night and will write from there. Be sure and let me know how Knox makes it. Hope rest of you are all well. In case we leave unexpectedly will write you if permitted. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey on September 28, 1917 M.W. Chandler Co “B” 1st Field Battalion Camp Alfred Vail Little Silver, N.J. Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill. Pike County Camp Alfred, Vail 26th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Golly Havent received any mail since being here. Thought sure would have received a letter before now. Everything going along OK. Awful chilly here but otherwise like it OK. Haven’t heard anything definite as to when we will go to France but were sure going. I’m anxious to get over there. 25 Signal Corps men and 3 officers left today for Franc. 2 come over to the Signal Corps Club nearly every night. Good way to pass time away. Have a dandy Victrola and piano. All kinds books and magazines to read. Been doing lot of fatigue since coming here getting camp straightened out. I haven’t been on a horse since leaving Texas but will go out tomorrow on drill. How are the children coming in school? Is the apple season over yet papa? How much does Grace’s baby weigh now? Lordy I’d love to see her. Did she enter the contest at the state fair? Well guess I’ll go down and finish my story. Am reading “The Dark Star” by Chambers. Have to be back in camp by 11 pm. We all drew overcoats yesterday. The sure are dandy. Will get rest of our winter clothing right away. Fitzjarrellz is over here with me tonight. You know he is the fellow I mentioned from White Hall. Does Ruby remember him? He used to go with Lucille Knox. Well must stop. Hurry up and write. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Fort Bliss, Texas on August 27, 1917 M.W. Chandler Co “B” 1st F.B.S.C. Ft. Bliss Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike County Co “B” 1st FBSC Ft. Bliss Texas Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received 2 letters from papa since returning from hike last Saturday evening and sure glad to get them. We went up into New Mexico on the hike. Had a dandy time. The company was split up and strung out through the country and we had continuos wire communication which we put up ourselves. I stayed on duty until midnight. A and C company were along too. A company is the Radio outfit. They put up their wireless apparatus and were in communication with El Paso all the time. They also had a wire strung to our camp and out company relayed all messages for El Paso to the Radio Co and they sent them by wireless to El Paso and Ft. Bliss. The man who brought Papa’s letter to me yesterday saw that it was from Pearl and asked me if I knew anyone there. I told him I lived there and he said he was from White Hall. He sleeps in tent next to mine. He is a step brother of Pearl Ellis and said he remembered Ruby and Pearl were at their house one time. His name is Fitzjarrellz. He is Corporal in the company and a nice fellow. He also knows Uncle Will’s family at Manchester and used to go with Lucille. Sure surprised me to meet him. Said he had been at Pearl lots of times playing ball and coming to boat shows and knows lots of the fellows around Pearl. He’s the only fellow I’ve ran across close from home. Our battalion played baseball with 2nd telegraph battalion yesterday and beat them 7 to 3. Captain was sure pleased. I had m picture taken in ball suit with another fellow. Just a Kodak. Will send you one soon as they are finished. Mama did you ever find the Kodaks in my suit case? Well its just 5 min until drill time and I’ve been writing this in an awful hurry so could get it off this am. Hope everybody is OK. Well write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed On: July 31, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ills Pike Co July 31st Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received letter from papa day before yesterday and one today and mighty glad to get them. General Representative of Russia was here today reviewing our troops. They had a big parade, about 12000 soldiers. Gee it sure looked pretty. As far back as I could see was column after column of soldiers. The signal corps was up toward the front, mounted. I had a pretty good house. By golly from the way paper reads the general had better beat it to Russia. Haven’t heard anything about how long will be here but couldn’t be surprised if didn’t stay here until next spring. It’s pretty warm here in day time but the night are always cool and have to cover up. Thane good thing. Awful glad the girls got their pass to Dunlap. I’ll write to them tonight in this company from Illinois. Sure seems good to see dome one from up there. What is Ralph doing? Hope he gets a job OK. I saw in paper where all the switches around Chad were on a strike. Hope it doesn’t delay the girls trip any. Well I’m in a hurry. Got to get ready to stand retreat. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Valentine on May 3, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike County Valentine 5/3rd 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Papa’s letter yesterday telling about passage of Conscription law and glad to get it. Guess I’ll stay with the job for a while but I’ll be darned if I wouldn’t like to come home for a while. if I thought there was any chance of having to go to war I’d go join Navy right now cause I’d rather be in navy than in army. Gee they’re sure billing out lots of stock here. Glad I’m off this agency. Got a letter from bess other day. Guess I’ll answer it tonight. Have plenty of time on this job now. Business pretty light. Gee it’s warm here at night now. They say mosquitoes are awful bad here in summer. Golly I’ll be it gets awful hot here in summer. How’s Coa fixed for operations now? Wouldn’t mind having a relay job up there. Maybe I’ll write J.L.M. for a job. I don’t like this County a bit. It’s too windy. Well I don’t know a thing to write so will stop. Hope everybody is OK. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Sierra Blanca January 2, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Sierra Blanca Jan 2, 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all, Got in here last night and went to work. Job is enough to keep a man busy but not so awful hard. Have a night helper to handle baggage, etc. So I don’t have to do anything but telegraphy and clear trains on Both TP na dishes. I’m plumb disgusted with board here. This place is about the size of Hillview and only one place in town to board and they sure treat a fellow awful. Have to carry wash hater to your room and no carpet and just about the poorest accommodations a man could find. Got 1 pork chap, 2 biscuits and a few friend potatoes for breakfast and thats all. I got up from the table hungry. Charge 30.00 month. I went to bed right after breakfast this morning and got up at exactly 10 minutes after seven and they told me I was too late for supper. Had to go buy a meal at Brown’s News. They know they have a fellow cinched and they don’t care how they treat them. I asked how much for just a room and they said just the same as for board and room. Now what do you think of that? I was figuring on getting my meals at restaurant but they queered that. It’s just plain hold up. They know you can’t get any place else to stay. If I stay here I’m sure going to get the Co to give me a box car or else build some kind of shanty and go to Baching. Can’t possibly get by for less than 35 to 40 a month for board the way it is now. If I get up after supper time I have to buy a meal and its hard to arrange sleep so can get breakfast and supper both cause I generally sleep 10 hours. Here I never eat more than 2 meals a day and they wont give me one of those if I happen to be late but they charge for it. Just the same – I told the manager what I thought abut it and he informed me I had the privilege of going some place else to board if I didn’t like it, which of course he knew I couldn’t get any place else. I’m going to every house intern tomorrow and see if I can’t get a room. Job pays 85 don’t get the 5.00 for handling mail as the night helper does that. Have lots trains to clear. Just twice as many as at Valentine cause here we get the Tp. trains too but don’t mind it as long as don’t have to handle baggage and mail. Pretty cool here. They say they have cold winters here. Well its now 11:45 s will stop and get things lined up to go to work. Work from 12 to 8. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Bess Chandler, To: Jeanette Knox Chandler and Family

From: Bess Chandler, To: Jeanette Knox Chandler and Family Mailed From: Morrisonville, Ill on November 6, 1916 Mrs. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill 1:40 Sunday PM Dearest Mama, Papa, and all I’ve just gotten cleaned up and thought I had better write. We had intended to go to Taylorville to a football game but something was the matter with the car. I did want to go so bad, but I’m going to Jane Carrie’s house to see about a party the Glee Club is to give Thursday night. I got along all OK with my recitation. I’ve had so many compliments on it. Nearly every time I’d see anyone who was there, they’d tell me how well I did. You know I told you that some Taylorville Ladies came down and organized a Hilson Ladie’s Club. Well Mrs. Pence was appointed as a committee of one to arrange for a Glee Club. She asked me and six other girls and we sang here, at Harvel, and at Taylorville. Our songs were real cute. We had one to the tun of My Little Girl – Oh Mr. Hughes you’re stock are dropping You are losing notes each day Oh Mr. Hughes we hear you’re knocking Tho you’re many miles away We read your talks in all the papers What you’ve promised not to do Oh Mr. Hughes we know you’re lonely But we can not note for you We had lots of other ones too and some Wilson yells. While we were at marvel a man gave us five dollars, so were going to have a party on it. We get to invite “our company” – Don’t know who to invite. The ladies of Tayloville treated us so nice and altogether we’ve had lots of fun. We’ll know Tuesday who is to be our president. I got my coat while I was at Taylorville. It’s brown velour. I did want a plush with brown fur trimming but they were too expensive. Hope I get to come home Thanksgiving but guess I wont if I don’t get a pass because my fare would be nearly six dollars. I’ll sure be home Xmas tho’ and get to stay from Dec 22 to Jan. Hope you are all well – I’m getting along fine at school. We had a program in my room day before yesterday. It was real good. The girls asked me to sin so I sang Little Gray Home in the West. We had company, too. Lorin has just offered Grace and I $0.15 each if we read something against Wilson in the Republican news paper. You couldn’t pick up a republican news paper without reading something against Wilson. In fact that’s about all it contains. I’m enclosing a five with which I want to pay my lodge dues for the past four or five months. Guess it wont come amiss if everything is as high as it is here. Flour is $3.00 a sack. You never did say whether or not you got the other $5.00 (which I borrowed to buy shoes in Springfield) I returned but I guess you did because you got the letter. I’ve got all of my debts paid now, Pearl Store, my board, Donohoo’s, Ruby, and you. I wrote to Mrs. Moultrie too – All I owe, now is a letter to Ruby and I’ll try to write it soon. Martin never did answer my letter. When you write tell him I’m looking for one. Lot’s of Love, Bess ...

November 6, 1916 · Post Cards

From: Grace Chandler, To: Jeanette Knox Chandler and Family

From: Grace Chandler Gourn, To: Jeanette Knox Chandler and Family Mailed From: Morrisonville, Illinois on October 27, 1916 Mrs. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Mrs. L.E. Gourn Morrisonville, Ill Morrionville 10/27 Dearest Folks, Rec’d Mama’s letter this am addressed to me and tho’t I’d write this after noon while I am resting. Washes pretty good washing this am. It is a fine bright day to dry them. Didn’t think when I got up I wanted to wash but everybody else around me was washing and just hated to see them get ahead. So Lorin stayed at home and helped me and with my electric washer and ringer was all thru by 10:30. Just got up at 7:30. Wasn’t that going some? But after I get all the scrubbing done I am pretty tired. Lorin went in the car to Raymond this afternoon and to Nokomio if he can make it. He will be back tonight. Sat we cleaned the house all up and washed windows inside and out. I don’t think I’ll do any general house cleaning. Washed the wood work around the windows and am going to rent a vacuum sweeper (electric) to clean the rugs and mattresses. All of us, including Glenn, Thelma, bots, Callie, Allie and Chas were at Mother’s for dinner and supper yesterday. Had dandy meals and a good time. Bess and I went uptown Sat pm to mail that mail you received and went on down to Callie’s and she insisted on us staying for supper. Lori came home on the 6:30 train and came on down there. We stayed until 11:3. Mother send me a bunch of green tomatoes today so I’ll have to work them into pickles this week. She made some dandy chow chow. Have brot my flower baskets inside. Think I’ll take out some of the best plants and pot them. Haven’t much room tho for flowers. That is, not a good sunny window to keep them in. Lorin got me a nice little trimmed oak sewing chair which is very comfortable. He bronzed my old gray shoes until now they look like new. What is the matter with Mr. Hanks that he has to be operated on? That’s too bad, hope he gets better. Hope Martin gets to stay at the place where he is all winter. He seems to be doing pretty well. Wonder why Bill Watters sold out in the picture show biz? Thot it was such a paying proposition? Maybe Annie was jealous of he and Roy being in together. Ha. Glad Ruth adn Basil have gone back . The course of true love never did run smooth. Would like for her to come up and visit us. I know Bess would enjoy having her. Bess wore her new dress, hat, and shoes yesterday and looked so nice. She’s so slender. Ha. I can’t help but notice it. Lorin has a nice new brown overcoat. He may be over there soon. I know he is going to make the territory around there either this week or next. Wish we could come home Thanksgiving. I expect Bess will if she gets enough days off. I want her to so she can visit with you all and then tell me all about it. Well this is all I can think of for now. Was glad to get Mama’s letter. Hope her cold is better. Would like to get weighed, believe I am getting heavier. Bess, Lorin, and I walked up town yesterday morning before going down to mothers. Much love to all. Write soon, Grace ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on September 18, 1916 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike Co Santa Margarita Sept 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Mama’s letter yesterday and haven’t heard from papa in a coons age. Sure was glad to get Mama’s letter. This is first time I’ve written for several days. Getting along OK and will be on this job at least a month longer and probably 2 months. Agent has gone to New York so regular 3rd trick man got his job leaving me here for while longer. Sure have a bunch of train orders to handle. Keeps me going sometimes. Getting pretty cool out there at night. Have to have a fire every night. See in the paper where they have rented the M.E. Church for Primary room. Thought they had built a new school house there or had added to the old one. See they’ve started on the ditch again. I’d like to see it. I’ve been going up to the school-man’s house nearly every night. We all went swimming other day. Gee they have swell schoolhouse here. Have piano, dance floor, opera house, and everything in it. Have some pretty swell scenery on the stage. It’s a single story building but has 4 rooms besides the opera and dance room. Sure is swell. Gee I sure was tickled when agent told me he was going to lay off 2 months. Felt pretty sure I’d get to stay on this job. Ruby is making a pretty good visit at Grace’s. Wish I was there. Well I must stop and get busy. Bell rings just about every 10 or 15 min for 9.00. Wish you’d all write a little more often. Lovingly, Martin ...
