From: Ruby Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Ruby Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Wichita, Kansas on October 23, 1912 Wichita, Kansas October 23, ’12 Dearest Papa, Mamma, and All: We received the children’s and Papa’s letters yesterday, Mamma’s registered and Bess’ letters today so I feel especially good tonight. I was awfully glad to get the $15.00 for Martin’s suit. Will order it Saturday, then he’ll get it in a week. After this month thought I will have gotten a good start and wont be so hard pressed for extra coin. We’ll get a pay day a few days earlier this month on account of the Topeka trip. Say Papa I wish you’d order it right away for Ill need it the 6th and thats a short time off. order it Via Rock Island as a special is chartered for the benefit of Wichita teachers and promises to get us there in about 14 and a half hours. I was glad Bess sent that music. I have those two you mentioned but can’t locate the song book. Will tell you later what I do and what happens. Bess, I was rather depending on you ordering my hat thru Edna, since you were going to pay me a little on my coat than $5.00. But if you can’t share it, I can wait until I get my next pay and get it here the day before I go to Topeka for I can’t get it right now. Form the way your letters read it seemed you didn’t get my last one for in it I asked about my hat and told you what I wanted also told Mamma on back of envelope it was ok about bed trade. If you can’t order hat thru Edna I’ll first get a sailor for I’d have to pay a sum for beaver and trim it like I want it. Ethyl and I rented a piano and it was moved in last Sat. so I feel so much more at home. I was first crazy about before I got it. I sure will put in the time. Martin learned to play “Rock of Ages” clear thru with both hands. He’s awfully proud of it. ha! He’s onto Mae’s now. I had to go to Teacher’s Association and a dinner afterwards so he went out there. I had him go to a “Delicatessen” right near us, for his supper and go out there afterwards. I haven’t seen her since Sunday but called her up this noon and she said she’d be down after school tomorrow. I did quite a washing last night and ironed this a.m. before I went to school so as to be ready for that down tonight. I wore my white veil and a tight red low made from the ribbon on my summer hat, put it right in the back. It looked good. I had several compliments. You know, my veil dress split in 3 or 4 places and I took it to Boston Store and showed them and they gave me $2.50 due bill for material. So I got a wide insertion and set it in which also lengthened it just enough. I’m quite proud of my handiwork. I still have $1.00 of that left. Not in money but due – bill. It will come in handy sometime. I am going to a show this week and wear my light blue dress. Ethyl had a fit over it. I agent been ad it on yet. I will be the first show I have seen, not even the princess. Sam is join got take me to Forum Friday night. I’m glad Grace got her a traveling bag. One needs it so often. Mine will come in handy at Topeka. Say, I fixed my blue dress up dandy so I can wear it all winter. I cleaned it and pressed it and put the waist back on so it looks most as good as new. I’ll clean my lavender so I can take both to the convention. Say, I have got a peachy coat. It’s a mixture and has big lapels of broadcloth. It’s as swell as any I’ve seen. It’s the bicle cloth, pretty rough mostly black, so a black hat will go well with it. Bess did you get your grey beaver trimmed with pink. I saw one down town, and it would be so pretty for you. Grace are you wearing your suit or your cast winter coat? You didn’t mention so I wondered. Your blue suit was pretty on you. The children’s letters were so cute. I can read them over and over and laugh every time. It sure tickles Martin to hear what Knox says. He says “Don’t you wish we had him here.” I have thought so often how much good it would do one of the little girls here with we while they were out of school so long. I want them to have music so bad. After awhile I’m going to ave Martin play. He wants to so bad and it seems easy for him. I forgot to tell you how I paid for my coat. Why since I’m a teacher I can run a bill, so whatever is absolutely necessary I can get there anytime. It came in handy for I needed it so bad when I got my dresses, I still wear my suit lots but its so hard to keep my waist ironed. I had them rough dried at the Steam Laundry also sheets, pillow cases and table cloths and Martin’s collars, came to $0.47. I’m going to have them laundered every 2 weeks, then do my own ironing. It’s sure worth that much and more to have them clean. I have some of the cutest pictures Mae and I made. Cut out magazine pictures and pasted them on gown mats. Some are of Sat. evening pasts. Adds lots to my room. I made some dandy corn fritters for supper last night and had some left for dinner today. It’s so handy having some thing already fixed for quick brunch at work. The mail usually comes at noon and I have a time popping from stove to letters getting them read. I’ll sure take time to write Aunt Sarah later also to Pearl. Girls I’ve had several cards from Irvin Pounds with his address – gentle hint! Guess I’ll write him too. You’ve asked so many time about color of Martin’s suit and I forget to answer. He wants brown mixture and its very popular too. A sort of red brown then he wants brown shoes before he comes home so of course I’ll be able to get them then. He takes real good care of his appearance – as good as possible. He can spend a good deal time at Mae’s or over to Sam’s (he usually does) while I am at Topeka and sorts rough about for his meals down here. I feel safe enough about leaving him so much with Sam. They’re pretty good roomies. And he likes Mrs. D.D. too she jollies with him so much. Don’t mention anything about being afraid to leave him in your letters but you might tell him to make the best of everything while I’m away. I know where he is every minute for he gets out a certain time and I know he doesn’t waste much getting here. he looks fine and never coughs one, since he had that cold. Grace, it’s about time you were writing again. I’d like to write lots more but will have to go to bed its 11:20 and I was up late last night. Guess I’ll write on the other side tho. Say Mrs. Mussenbach must want her fame as a poctiss to spread abroad. I saw two in the paper last week. Guess I’ll send Carrie a card, and put my address on it. Hers is just Woodriver but I’ll put c/o Washington School, Cleveland Ave. I’m rather anxious to know the outcome of the settlement, so is Mae. Art asked Mae what she was going to do with her half. He’s pretty busy making Xmas presents. Guess he don’t want the worry of it right at the last. Must close. Write as often as you can. It’s a deal to look forward to after school hours to expect mail. I think thats why Martin rushes home so. Love to All, Ruby ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailing Address: Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike County Return Address: M. Chandler COB 1st BSC Mammouth Parking 9 am Indianapolis, Mo. 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, & all En route to Mammouth Park, Mo. Cmae through K.C. & St. Louis both but was only in each place 15 minutes and was way down in yards. We weren’t allowed to leave train at all. Just now pulling into Maple. Intended to write you from KC but no chance. We came through St. Louis at 11 pm last night. Rock bends El Paso to St. Louis and then St. Louis to destination. Should reach New Jersey about night. All the trains are taking siding for us and we have had a clear track since leaving Mt. Bliss. Delayed occasional by broken tunnel on one of the car horses and then we have to unload them every 3 hours and let them rest 8 hours. All the little towns in Missouri and Kansas had word of our coming and school was dismissed and they were all down to station with flags to wave at use. One girl passed out a basket of cigarettes. from way they are rushing us am inclined to think we will go on over to France although some say we are going to camp in NJ for winter. We have dandy pullman and good meals. Having fine trip. Didn’t come through Wichita. Wanted to bad too. Came through Hutchinson, Kans. Well I’m in hurry so can mail this here in Maples cause don’t know when we will stop again. Will write you just as soon as get to New Jersey and give you my address. Love to all Martin (over) P.S. Suppose you get the Battalion picture my picture isn’t a bit good. Can hardly be recognized. Am in the sixth grow from the right and third row right next to the big black horse on cart. ...

December 16, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Little Silver, New Jersey October 18 Oct 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received papas letter yesterday and Bess’ dandy letter today and mighty glad to get them. I came over other night and wrote but forgot to mail it so guess it has been quite a while since you’ve heard from me. The 11th Reserve battalion left for France this morning. We got our lockers back this am also got stoves so guess we will camp here this winter from looks of things but of course can’t tell for sure. I think they intended to send one battalion over and hadn’t decided who to send hence our preparedness but now since the 11th have gone we’re likely to stay here for some time. I’ve been getting the Pike Country Republican lately. Who’s sending it? It has news from boys at Camp Taylor. Saw Wade’s and Jesse’s picture in it. Tell Parish I’ll write the Tribune a letter relative Army Life as far as I knew. Don’t know anything about it except Signal Corps Branch. Awful glad to hear Knox’s arm is healing. Hope he doesn’t fall from some place and break it again before it mends. We’ve been out on target range 3 days out of this week and go again tomorrow. I can shoot pretty well. Scored 76 out of 100 first time. I even tho one of the automatic pistols. Lots pretty good shots in this company. Is Aunt Sarah still at our house? They are offering liberty bonds to our company on installment plans. We have about 75 men and they’re taken $4200.00 so far. I can’t take any account the allotment home. Have o pay at rates $10.00 per month and thats all I’m getting. By the way, you’ll get first payment 1st of December. Captain said it was paid one month behind June all the time and then you’ll get the last one a month after I’m out of army. Gee Margaret is getting rather popular. Having bunch of fellows wanting to call on her. It’s pretty chilly here. heavy frosts each morning. Well I’ll stop now and go back to camp. Pretty tired tonight. On our feet all day today fixing stoves for the company. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on July 22 Santa Margarita July 22nd Dearest Papa and All, Just received letters from you, mama, Lucerne and Margaret fe minutes ago and was sure glad to get the. Wish they would come that way all the time. I wrote yo Mama few minutes ago and wrote to her day before yesterday in Morrisonville so guess she will get it soon after she gets there. Getting along OK on the job. Coping about 50 train orders last night and had 43 Expense Bills. Handle mail or 3 trains and sell tickets for 3 trains so there’s generally enough to keep me busy. Like the job fine though. Theres lots of fellows here wanting to lay off and can’t get relief. M en are awful scarce but in the winter time they close so many offices that it throws a whole bunch of men on extra board but extra work looks pretty good for this winter and if I earn to handle an agency by that time will be assured of almost steady work. These agencies pay big money too. Do you and Knox get any fish? That was a pretty good string you caught the other day. Bet Knox was tickled to yank them out. Wish I was there so could go squirrel hunting few times. Gee the girls sure are bathing lots berries. Hope Ruby is feeling better. Golly I haven’t heard a piano for so long I’ve forgotten how one sounds. Hope Ralph will be able to go back to work soon. Too bad Micky had to take sick. Well the train is coming now that I want to get this off on so will close. Wish all of you would write. Hope everyone is OK. Will send Annie a card right away. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: France on March 10 France Mar 10th Dearest Folks, Will write just few lines tonight while have good chance. Nothing new to write. Have received no mail yet. Suppose it will be several months before you can get a letter to me at this address. Requires quite a while to get an answer to a letter. Have moved since last writing. Would like to tell you of our locations. Things are pretty warm here. Been having some very nice weather here for past week. Hope it continues. I’ve been feeling very good. Get plenty to eat. Lovingly, Martin P.S. Dont have any of my letters put in “Tribune.” ...

March 10, 1900 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: France on February 28 Dearest Folks, Have been traveling about quite a bit since last writing you. Was transferred to the 117th F.B.S.C. Feb 12. Like the organization very well and if I had my mail, clothes, etc from the 1st FB would just as soon stay with this organization. My service record is still with my old company. Don’t know when I’ll get a pay. Haven’t received any pay since October. Would like very much to get the mail you’ve already sent to me at 1st FB. But don’t know whether or not it will be forwarded here. Been having some very rainy weather the past week. Guess this is the rainy season. First I’ve seen since being over here. Would like to tell you of our whereabouts but of course that’s forbidden. Am feeling very good. Only hope you are all feeling as well. Must stop for this time. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin Address: Pvt. Martin W. Chandler Co ‘C” 117 F.B.D.C. A.E.F. Via New York ...
