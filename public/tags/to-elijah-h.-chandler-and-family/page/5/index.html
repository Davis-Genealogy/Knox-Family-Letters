From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Langtry, Texas on May 9, 1917 Langtry, Tex 5/9th 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Got her evening of 7th. This sure is some town. One store and 2 or 3 restaurants. Not a very heavy job. Pays $87.50 and commissions all told ought to make it pay 100 but I’ll only be here two or three days longer at least. I can stand on platform and throw across the Rio Grande into Old Mexico. Only about a hundred yards to River. Saw a whole bunch of Mexicans over there yesterday. Don’t know what they were doing. I telegraph from 9 am to 3 pm. Express Commission ran $8.00 last month. Ha. He said it was a very poor month. Don’t have very much to do. Have oil tanks here and have to contend with that but its nothing like Valentine. I kinda wish he hadn’t out me on Valentine agency cause now I’ll have to be running around working relief agencies and can’t stay on my regular job and its more expensive to be running around here and there. Do hope Margaret is well. Awful glad to hear Lucerne made her grades. How did Knox make it? Too bad Margaret couldn’t take examination. Did I mention receiving Papas and Mamas letters enclosing Bess’? Anyway I did and mighty glad to get them. Haven’t heard anything from Cra yet but expect to in few days. Don’t suppose I could get a pass up there anyway. I got a letter from MK 2 and they said they would be glad to furnish me pass San Antonio to St. Louis provided it was ok with me that they notify this road they had issued such transportation as required by I.C.C. Upon inquiry I found out that if I get pass from MK 2 that the RHASA will blacklist me from their road. They don’t allow their employees to write for their own passes without taking up with them so that knocks me out of pass cause I don’t want to have a bad record here. I don’t see why in the dickens they should care who I get pass from. Guess if they wont furnish transportation I’ll ride the cabooses. Wouldn’t take me very long to get there. Sure will be glad when this war is over. I’m going to work in Western Union this summer sure. It’s good for $125.00 anytime. They have established an 8 hour day and overtime on Sundays now. That makes W.U. pretty good. Well must stop and Get busy. Better write me at Valentine. Expect to be back there by time you get this but if go someplace else can have my mail forwarded. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Valentine on May 3, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Pike County Valentine 5/3rd 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Papa’s letter yesterday telling about passage of Conscription law and glad to get it. Guess I’ll stay with the job for a while but I’ll be darned if I wouldn’t like to come home for a while. if I thought there was any chance of having to go to war I’d go join Navy right now cause I’d rather be in navy than in army. Gee they’re sure billing out lots of stock here. Glad I’m off this agency. Got a letter from bess other day. Guess I’ll answer it tonight. Have plenty of time on this job now. Business pretty light. Gee it’s warm here at night now. They say mosquitoes are awful bad here in summer. Golly I’ll be it gets awful hot here in summer. How’s Coa fixed for operations now? Wouldn’t mind having a relay job up there. Maybe I’ll write J.L.M. for a job. I don’t like this County a bit. It’s too windy. Well I don’t know a thing to write so will stop. Hope everybody is OK. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Eliojah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: El Paso, Texas on June 10, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois St. Blanca January 10, 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all; Received 2 letters from Papa today. One via valentine and I was glad to get them both. Didn’t think you’d be able to get one to St. Blanca so quick. Nothing new to write. Getting along O.K. I called up both last night and asked them what they wanted me to do in case one called while I was working with other and they said just use my own judgement and if I pay no attention to them they get sore. I’m hitting the ball here pretty lively. Keep busy every minute of the night. The depo I trick open copied a 19 for a IP 2/5 train this pm to meet another IP No. 2 train at Fort Hancock. No. 2 got to Fort Hancock and waited 2 hours for the 2nd 5 to show up but they didn’t so he called up Dispr. on phone and went on. 2nd No. 5 was in a blind siding down the line. They claimed they didn’t get the order. Disp. came after open about it and open looked for his clearance and he had it non clearance so it will be up to the train creed. Well, must stop. Will write again soon. Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: England on January 9, 1917 Somewhere in England Jan 9th Dearest Folks, Thought I’d write few lines tonight while have time but can’t tell you much except that I’m feeling OK and had a pleasant voyage. It’s pretty cold here but not any worse than New Jersey. Am not allowed to mention hardly anything so don’ expect to learn anything about the war from me. Rode on an English train other night. They’re a great deal different from ours. They are built in compartments. Each compartment sealing 8 men (3rd class). No way of getting from one compartment to another except by getting off and entering another. Just like traveling in a carriage. Had a good stateroom on the steamer and didn’t get a bit seasick. I’ll write you again in few days and don’t worry if you don’t hear from me regularly. Hope you are all well- Lots Love Martin Address Exactly Like This: Martin W. Chandler Co B 126 Field Bin Dig Corps American Exp Forces ...

January 9, 1917 · Post Cards

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Mailed From: Sierra Blanca January 2, 1917 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill Sierra Blanca Jan 2, 1917 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all, Got in here last night and went to work. Job is enough to keep a man busy but not so awful hard. Have a night helper to handle baggage, etc. So I don’t have to do anything but telegraphy and clear trains on Both TP na dishes. I’m plumb disgusted with board here. This place is about the size of Hillview and only one place in town to board and they sure treat a fellow awful. Have to carry wash hater to your room and no carpet and just about the poorest accommodations a man could find. Got 1 pork chap, 2 biscuits and a few friend potatoes for breakfast and thats all. I got up from the table hungry. Charge 30.00 month. I went to bed right after breakfast this morning and got up at exactly 10 minutes after seven and they told me I was too late for supper. Had to go buy a meal at Brown’s News. They know they have a fellow cinched and they don’t care how they treat them. I asked how much for just a room and they said just the same as for board and room. Now what do you think of that? I was figuring on getting my meals at restaurant but they queered that. It’s just plain hold up. They know you can’t get any place else to stay. If I stay here I’m sure going to get the Co to give me a box car or else build some kind of shanty and go to Baching. Can’t possibly get by for less than 35 to 40 a month for board the way it is now. If I get up after supper time I have to buy a meal and its hard to arrange sleep so can get breakfast and supper both cause I generally sleep 10 hours. Here I never eat more than 2 meals a day and they wont give me one of those if I happen to be late but they charge for it. Just the same – I told the manager what I thought abut it and he informed me I had the privilege of going some place else to board if I didn’t like it, which of course he knew I couldn’t get any place else. I’m going to every house intern tomorrow and see if I can’t get a room. Job pays 85 don’t get the 5.00 for handling mail as the night helper does that. Have lots trains to clear. Just twice as many as at Valentine cause here we get the Tp. trains too but don’t mind it as long as don’t have to handle baggage and mail. Pretty cool here. They say they have cold winters here. Well its now 11:45 s will stop and get things lined up to go to work. Work from 12 to 8. Love to all, Martin ...

From: Grace Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Grace Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family Sent From: Morrisonville on October 1, 1916 Morrisonville 10/1/16 Dearest Papa, Mama, & all: – Guess you think it’s about time I was writing – Bess and I just got back from Sunday School. We took a spurt this am and got ready and went to the Presbyterian SS and everyone treated us so nice we are glad we went. bess is correcting some papers and making out some examination papers and I have dinner on cooking. Did think I would come in and play the graphonolia but thot I would be making better use of my time if I write home. Ruby left yesterday morning and we sure miss her. Bess and i took her to the train, had to get up at 6 am so we could have breakfast and get to the depot by 6:58 am. Bess and i then came home, cleaned the house real good and she had to go to a teachers reading circle at 2 pm. I dusted and scrubbed after she left took a bath and dressed up again and went to meet her. Got there in time to hear part of the work. We walked up town again went to Callie’s and she insisted on us staying and having supper. So Lorin came in on the 6:30 pm train and we went to meet him and all took supper there. Then went to the picture show. Lorin bought me the most beautiful purple velvet tricornered hat for my birthday, he said, but that isn’t until the 6th of next month. So he was in plenty of time – Ha. My white coat just got back this am from the cleaners, so you make know I wore my new hat and white coat to S.S. and felt quite dressed up. Bess cleaned her lack suit yesterday and wore my other purple hat. Our electric washer came yesterday pm and Lorin got up early this morning and had it in working order by the time we got up. It sure us a peach and I’m tickled we have it – will sure save my back. Lorin is going to Taylorville to get out car today, had to leave it up there the last time he was up there, on account of some man running into him and busting it up some. Will cost about $30 to get it fixed up again. Lorin sued him for $200 – the trail comes up next week. Hope he gets what it will cost us. The car is alright now so he is going to get it today. I am cooking apples whole not peeled, with such red skin, also sweet potatoes, kraut pork & wieners. Have raw tomatoes with mayonnaise dressing for dinner. How is papa? You said he was not feeling well when you last wrote. Hope is better by now. Guess Margaret & Lucerne will have a great time in St. Louis. Lorin said I could go, but I don’t believe I want to. It would be different if he and Bess could both go. I was surprised Leta & John stayed in Pearl to spend their honeymoon. Guess they have gone to St. Louis by now. Mon. P.M. Guess I’ll finish this and get it mailed. After dinner yesterday we drove to Taylorville with Oren Bloxom and another fellow, attended a football game, and drove our own car back home. Oren Bloxom came back with us – He’s a Taylorville boy, and is going to be a lawyer. Bess entertained him until his train time. I was so tired, Lorin and I went to bed. I had lunch for all. Lorin forwarded two letters to Ruby this a.m. I told him to send them to Pearl I was afraid she wouldn’t get them if we sent them to Granite City. I pulled the pears, Ruby will be glad to know and have just finished two gallons of pear butter. I had them on hand for over two weeks, so you may be sure I’m glad they’re cooked up. Lorin is going to put up the base burner this afternoon. It isn’t very cold yet but he thought he’d better put it up while he had time. Bess said at noon that she must write home. I told her I had a letter started. She has four sets of papers to correct this evening after she comes home. She’ll be pretty busy this week with giving examinations and correcting papers. I wish papa could come up if only for a few days, might do him good and we would be tickled to death to have him. Some of you could get a pass and come up anytime. Well this is all I can think of for now. Lorin will be down around that part of the country next week, I suppose. Write when you can, we’re always ticked to hear from home. Much love to all, Lovingly, Grace Tues. A.M. – Am sure going to get this mailed this morning. We have a train out of here for St. Louis at 3:37 P.M. and Lorin was just saying at the breakfast table that we could go down and see the Veiled Prophet tonight and get home on the midnight train if Bess could get her Supt. to teach for her half hour. So no telling we may be there tonight ourselves. The matter is to be settled at noon after Bess talks with the Supt. and Lorin is to study it over. Write soon. Grace ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on September 20, 1916 M.W. Chandler Southern Pacific Company Santa Margarita, Cal. Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Ill. Pike Co. Santa Margarita, Spet. 20 Dearest Papa, Mama, and all, Received a letter from Papa yesterday and a long on today and was sure glad to get them. Gee they sure are doing some business back there. Think I told you in other letter I’d be here a month longer and possibly two. Glad to hear Ralph is back at work. I don’t want to work on era very bad but I’d like awful well to be closer to home. Gee I wish I could see some of you . I sold a ticket to Waterburg Conn last night – $72.40- I didn’t have any ticket on hand so I wired Sam Luis Abirro to send me one and gave him the routing and he sent it up all made out. Excursion tickets are on sale the year round. Every Friday and Saturday have reduced rates to nearly all the places along the line. Can get a ticket to Chicago and return for $65.00. Sure have some powerful engines on this line. They’re all oil burners. Haven’t a coal burner on the line. I stuck a first class passenger train 5 minutes yesterday morning account putting “9th” for “September” – They might give me 10 days for it – They’re awful strict. They all say that if you can work for the S.P. you can work for any road in the country. They sure enforce their rules. I was in a hurry to make out the clearance as train was ready to leave and without thinking I put 9/20 instead of September 20 – That’s OK on any other road but this one. It takes about 3 years to land a job on this road. But if extra work is good I think I’ll stick. Of course I’d probably lay off and come home before 3 years. But these are pretty good jobs and I want to land something steady if I can. I don’t have to fill lamps or lanterns or sweep out or handle baggage or anything like that. Have a baggage boy to do that. All stations have a baggage boy. All I have to do is sell tickets, copy orders, and put on mail. Sure copy a bunch of orders and seek quite a few tickets. Well must stop. Hope to hear from you all soon- I’ll write more often Lovingly, Martin ...

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler and Family

From: Martin Chandler, To: Elijah H. Chandler Mailed From: Santa Margarita, CA on September 18, 1916 Mr. E.H. Chandler Pearl, Illinois Pike Co Santa Margarita Sept 18th Dearest Papa, Mama, and all Received Mama’s letter yesterday and haven’t heard from papa in a coons age. Sure was glad to get Mama’s letter. This is first time I’ve written for several days. Getting along OK and will be on this job at least a month longer and probably 2 months. Agent has gone to New York so regular 3rd trick man got his job leaving me here for while longer. Sure have a bunch of train orders to handle. Keeps me going sometimes. Getting pretty cool out there at night. Have to have a fire every night. See in the paper where they have rented the M.E. Church for Primary room. Thought they had built a new school house there or had added to the old one. See they’ve started on the ditch again. I’d like to see it. I’ve been going up to the school-man’s house nearly every night. We all went swimming other day. Gee they have swell schoolhouse here. Have piano, dance floor, opera house, and everything in it. Have some pretty swell scenery on the stage. It’s a single story building but has 4 rooms besides the opera and dance room. Sure is swell. Gee I sure was tickled when agent told me he was going to lay off 2 months. Felt pretty sure I’d get to stay on this job. Ruby is making a pretty good visit at Grace’s. Wish I was there. Well I must stop and get busy. Bell rings just about every 10 or 15 min for 9.00. Wish you’d all write a little more often. Lovingly, Martin ...
