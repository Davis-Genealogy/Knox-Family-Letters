From: Betty Ann Tilley, To: Amelia J. Wiggins Mailed From: Topeka, Kansas on February 24, 1942 Mrs. B. A. Tilley 1312 Harrison Topeka, Kansas Mrs. Amelia J. Wiggins 725 Waverly Route 5 Columbus, Ohio Tokepa, Kansas Feb. 24-42 Dearest Auntie, It isn’t because we forgot you dear that we do not write but I am so busy all the time. This house is so big and always extra work to be done. Bertie comes usually of noon lunch and I try to write Harold every day. He is in Watertown, South Dakota. Mother not very well. She occasionally dries dishes for me but that is about all she can do. Hire very little help. Bert, Louie, and Harold are all pretty well. I keep fair. Hope darling you and your little pet are both all right. Wish you would write us a card. Love, Niece Betty 1312 Harrison ...

From: Betty Tilley, To: Amelia J. Wiggins Mailed From: Topeka, Kansas on June 30, 1941 Mrs. Amelia J. Wiggins 725 Waverly Route 5 Columbus, Ohio Mrs. H.W. Tilley Hotel Paumee Merik Platte, Nebraska Dearest Aunt Millie, We are in a hurry, ready to leave Topeka for Merik Platte Nebraska, Hotel Paumee. Enclosed is a letter Maifer wrote you some time ago. It is almost impossible for her to write as her vision is so bad. She can not read at all. Maifer is going to Merik Platte with me. Shall write you again when hot in such a rush to leave town. Have a four hundred mile drive ahead of us. We hope you are feeling better dear. I am awfully bust all the time. Together with caring for Maifer and this big house it is so much for me. We are all pretty well however. Hope to hear from you, maybe you know some one who will send us a card to The Paumee Htel at Merik Platte Nebraska. Love from both of us. Your devoted niece Betty (Mrs. H.W. Tilley) ...

From: Lucy Clarkin, To: Amelia J. Wiggins

From: Lucy Clarkin, To: Amelia J. Wiggins Mailed From: San Diego on May 21, 1938 Mrs. Lucy C. Clarkin 2062 Venice St. Ocean Beach San Diego, California Mrs. A.J. Wiggins 725 Waverly St. K-5 Columbus, Ohio Mrs. A.J. Wiggins May 21, 1938 Dear, dear, dear, friend, Here I am starting an air mail letter to you. We are having real May weather here now. Some fog and light showers at night. Just now the radio is reporting the crash of an air plane against a mountain top and burning, fatal to all on board. My neighbor is laid up for repairs. She had a pretty complete Xray. She would like to have me with her all the time but I stay away when I know someone else is there. Rheumatism and sciatica are bothering me some. Pretty painful if I do not take Iodide of Potash regularly. 1/4 of an ounce in 4 ounce of water. A tablespoon after meals is the regular dose but I take 2 teaspoons twice a day and get along pretty well. I have not heard from Celia. She has probably joined up with her children. Maybe she and her children are probably leading regular Indian lives and living on fish and pinion nuts. I heard a number of air planes roaring overhead during the night. Air mail week and not good weather for flying but things may be clearing up. I am enclosing an Easter Card I mislaid and did not send. California poppies are out in my yard, very beautiful. My doctor and my dentist are waiting to finish my job. I am intending to write to the grand-daughter-in-law of a 91 year old cousin of my mother an air mail letter with a dime for the old man. Love & much love, Lucy C. Clarkin Had a severe stomach and heart attack last night. Bad indigestion. Am sending Celia an Air Mail letter asking her to write to you. ...
