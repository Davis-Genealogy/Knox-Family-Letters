From: Ruby Chandler, To: Bess Chandler

From: Ruby Chandler, To: Bess Chandler Miss Bess Chandler 116 Spaulding Pl. Jacksonville, Ill. Written on the Front: Artist G.E.C. Ain’t I a dandy one? 1/10/10 – 4:45 p.m. Tried so hard to see you. Train whistled just as I left school, so I went on home, then went on to the depot and found the train still there. I quickened my steps some, if that could be done and missed it by two or three lengthy strides. 4:35 Keep this. Don’t she look cute or rather – charming? Ruby ...
