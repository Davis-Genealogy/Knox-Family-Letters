From: Bertha, To: Jeanette Knox

From: Bertha, To: Jeanette Knox Postcard Info: 10X64 Made in USA Dear Nettie I think you will be interested to know that I have completed the quilts as I had planned and they are not bad looking quilts. I had Mrs. Grafford do another one, and she did much better work than on the first. While I put every stitch in the one for Mildred, have added two more on top of match and this will be a real surprise to Pauline, as she was so sure I’d never get her’s done. We will spend Christmas day with Milgred and Paul’s family. ...
